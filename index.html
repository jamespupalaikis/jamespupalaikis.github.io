James Pupalaikis

Machine learning engineer. Physicist by training.

I'm James. I build machine learning systems for a living, currently at IBM, where most of my work is turning other people's messy data problems into something that runs in production and keeps running after I've moved on to the next one.

Before that I was a physicist, which mostly taught me to be comfortable around things that don't work yet. I write mainly in Python. The parts of machine learning I actually like are the ones that touch real systems and real people, which tend to be the unglamorous parts.

Why does this page exist?

A justification for its own existence

Reasonable question. The honest first answer is that the alternative was leaving a half-finished page with a broken image sitting on this domain indefinitely, and that had gone on long enough.

The practical answer: I wanted somewhere to keep my resume that isn't a PDF decaying in someone's inbox, and somewhere to put the occasional small project that has no other natural home.

The less practical answer is that buying a domain and pointing it at a folder of hand-written HTML is one of the last genuinely simple things left on the internet. No CMS, no framework, no analytics, no JavaScript. A few files served exactly as they were written. I like it that way, and that's most of the reason this site looks like it does.
